#4471 · three problems in one ticket

owner pavani06 open reviewed 2026-11-10 · 1 days ago

The desk merged every report of a booking date gone wrong into #4471 as one issue. Read by tenant, the way the desk’s own rules read them, those reports hold three problems, and each needs a fix of its own.

The three problems

problemreports
parser379
hint33
one-day5
  • parser: the shared date parser takes the first number of a date as the month. Tenants whose locale writes the day first get day and month swapped, on every channel: the app, email, and the booking form whose hint reads day first.
  • hint: the other booking form prints a month-first hint (MM/DD/YYYY) under its date field. On a day-first tenant, people type the month first because the hint tells them to, and the form reads the field day first, so day and month swap. Only reports sent through that form have this cause, and fixing the parser does not fix it.
  • one-day: reports the desk read as a booking moved by one day, or as one meeting showing a different weekday in different places. A swap of day and month does not explain them. The desk files them under parser by tenant, so the parser count includes them; they still need a look of their own.

Where the counts come from

Both tables are built at render time from the ticket system’s export, with the desk’s rules in src/lib/kb/causes.ts: one report per id, filed by its tenant’s locale and booking form, not by the tag intake gave it.
